# What is recordable now (D02-06, 2026-10-27)

One full live round on the withRpcRetry fixture can be recorded today from `pnpm dev:all` in a
browser: pick S3, see the plain-code questions, bet, watch the server seal your bets (hashes and
server times appear), watch "Muse Spark 1.3 (muse-spark-1.3) is placing its bets…" turn into "has
sealed 3 bets" with its own hashes and no values (about 3–5 s, a live Meta call), press "Break it",
and see the real run's facts, the verdict table with a column for You and one for Muse Spark 1.3
(muse-spark-1.3), six seal checks done by the page, and Spark's reasons in its panel. Two things to
know before recording: on S3 Spark bet 5 / 12000 / throws in all 4 live calls and matched every time,
so if you bet the same the clip shows a tie, and if you bet differently only your column shows
"didn't match"; and the withRpcRetry card must keep its "Pre-existing" label on screen (the code
predates the hackathon). Not recordable yet: the Investigator finding (Oct 30; the finding is still
the hand-written fixture), the log strip, the machines, and anything Moonwell.
